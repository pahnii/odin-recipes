Chocolate Pie

Description

Chocolate pie is a rich and creamy dessert that features a chocolate filling in a flaky pie crust, often topped with whipped cream or meringue.

Ingredients

  • Pie crust
  • Chocolate
  • Milk or cream
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Butter
  • Vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Prepare the pie crust and place it in a pie dish.
  3. In a saucepan, melt the chocolate with milk or cream over low heat, stirring until smooth.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk together sugar and eggs until well combined.
  5. Gradually add the melted chocolate mixture to the egg mixture, stirring constantly to prevent curdling.
  6. Pour the chocolate filling into the prepared pie crust.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven for 30-35 minutes, or until the filling is set.
  8. Remove from the oven and let it cool completely before serving. Optionally, top with whipped cream or meringue before serving.
